  - icon: "fas fa-plug"
    title: "Loads"
    content: "Consumption is modeled as distinct time profiles linked to the behavior of the different energy users, so that peaks, operating cycles and the actual scope for flexibility are clearly distinguished."

  - icon: "fas fa-charging-station"
    title: "Electrification"
    content: "New electrical loads are simulated from actual use rather than aggregate estimates. Mobility, processes and climate control enter the model with specific constraints and operating patterns."

  - icon: "fas fa-battery-full"
    title: "BESS"
    content: "The BESS is represented by power, energy, SoC, efficiency and operating constraints. The control strategy determines when to charge, discharge or preserve capacity for subsequent scenarios."
